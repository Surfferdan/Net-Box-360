I also have one suggestion that I think will pay off later: define a ConsoleSession object as the centerpiece of the whole platform. Instead of each subsystem tracking its own state, everything—account, Xenia process, CloudMorph stream, virtual display, connected players, controller assignments, and stream URLs—belongs to one ConsoleSession. That gives you a single source of truth and makes features like reconnecting, resuming, spectator mode, or multi-device play much easier to add later.
